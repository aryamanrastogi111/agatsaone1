# EasyTouch Rhythm Band Shopify Theme v7

This version is a proper Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme export.

## What changed
- Homepage is now `templates/index.json`, not a hardcoded `index.liquid` page.
- The page is split into market-style draggable Shopify sections.
- Every major image area has an `image_picker` setting in the Theme Editor.
- Cards, features, FAQ rows, timeline rows, food rows, and colour swatches are Shopify blocks that can be added, removed, reordered, and hidden.
- The buy box uses a selected Shopify product and adds to cart/checkout through Shopify cart.

## After uploading
1. Go to Online Store > Themes > Customize.
2. Open the Home page.
3. Pick the product in the Rhythm hero and Buy box sections.
4. Replace images from each section's image picker if needed.
5. Drag, hide, duplicate, or remove sections from the left sidebar.

Bundled fallback images still show even before you upload your own images, but the settings are editable.
